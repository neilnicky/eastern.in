## Rules applied

- Each product shows **its own** pack image. The best available original is used: 94 of 141 have a hi-res "-scaled" upload, and the rest use the 548×600 product-page image.
- Pack cut-outs remove the white studio background only. A border flood-fill plus colour-to-alpha keeps soft shadows, and a convex-hull guard protects white label panels. Packaging pixels are not edited, recoloured or retouched.
- Campaign banners and cookbook cards keep their baked-in text, so they are never cropped through it on display. Banner cards keep their aspect ratio at every width. Cookbook cards crop to the photo half (`object-position` set per card side).
- Low-resolution archive images render at their intrinsic size or smaller.
- Images are optimised at build time by Astro (`astro:assets`: responsive widths, WebP, lazy loading below the fold).
- Brand marks: `src/assets/brand/eastern-logo.svg` was traced with potrace from `2025/02/Eastern-Logo-withTM.png` as three colour layers, and checked visually against the PNG. `flag-shape.svg` is the flag silhouette from the same trace.
- Downloads are served from `public/wp-content/uploads/…`, the original paths.
